# Product work

Use only the domain guidance needed for the request. This skill complements the main research or analysis workflow; it does not create a mandatory product process. Keep the output sized to the decision: a calculation, short recommendation, comparison, or plan may be enough. Do not force a template or fill sections that do not help.

Separate observed evidence from assumptions, interpretation, and recommendations. Mark unknowns plainly. Do not present synthetic interviews, illustrative scenarios, or model-generated examples as customer evidence. Do not invent market counts, prices, rates, targets, or benchmark values; calculate from supplied or sourced inputs and show assumptions and uncertainty.

Load selectively:
- For market boundaries, alternatives, and TAM/SAM/SOM, read [references/market.md](references/market.md).
- For product direction, stage-appropriate choices, and experiments, read [references/strategy.md](references/strategy.md).
- For metric definitions, cohorts, and unit economics, read [references/metrics.md](references/metrics.md).

When research is needed, use the available research workflow for sourcing and evidence handling; this skill supplies product-specific questions and checks, not a competing research procedure.
