# Strategy and stage

Choose the next action from the product's actual stage and uncertainty; the stages below are reference points, not a required funnel.

- **Idea:** test a concrete problem, its frequency and severity, and current workarounds. Look for real user evidence and counterevidence before recommending a build.
- **MVP:** define the riskiest hypothesis, the smallest useful test, observable user behavior, and a continue/change/stop condition. Record consequential product and technical tradeoffs.
- **Launch:** define activation, useful behavior, and return in terms specific to the product before interpreting signups or enthusiasm.
- **Growth:** reduce dependence on one founder by making ownership, knowledge transfer, and quality observable and repeatable.

Separate facts from predictions and state what observation could change the recommendation. Avoid universal numerical thresholds for retention, survey response, or growth: use product-specific baselines, evidence, and constraints. Simulated interviews or personas can help generate questions but are not evidence of actual customer behavior.

A useful strategy output may be a concise choice with rationale, alternatives, key assumption, and next test. Add detail only when it changes the decision.
